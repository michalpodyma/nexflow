Nexflow — Email Signatures

Gmail-ready HTML email signatures for all Nexflow team members. Each signature uses Nexflow brand colors (navy #0F2040, cyan #00C2D1) and is optimized for Gmail compatibility.

How to install in Gmail

  1. Find your signature below and click "Copy HTML"
  2. Open Gmail → Settings (gear icon) → See all settings
  3. Go to the General tab → scroll to Signature section
  4. Click "Create new" and give it a name (e.g. "Nexflow")
  5. Click the <> (Insert HTML) button in the signature editor toolbar
  6. Paste the copied HTML and click OK
  7. Scroll down and click "Save Changes"

Note: The logo image requires an internet connection to display. It is hosted at nexflow.work/nexflow-logo-email.svg.
